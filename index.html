01

the beginning

And somehow, here we are...

Some of the most beautiful things in life do not begin with a moment you recognise as important.

Ours was like that.

It began quietly, almost carelessly, with conversations that were never meant to become memories, little jokes that were never meant to be remembered, and moments that seemed ordinary while we were living them.

Neither of us knew where any of it was going. There was no promise, no certainty, no little sign telling us that this person would soon become someone we would care about so deeply.

We simply kept talking. Then we kept finding reasons to talk again. And somehow, without either of us noticing exactly when it happened, those small moments began to mean something more.

You slowly became a familiar part of my days. Someone I wanted to tell things to. Someone whose voice could change the feeling of an ordinary moment. Someone I would find myself thinking about long after our conversations had ended.

Looking back now, I think that is what makes the beginning of us so beautiful. Nothing had to happen all at once. It was built quietly, one conversation, one laugh, one little memory at a time.

And then, somewhere along the way, you stopped being someone I had simply met and became someone I never wanted to lose.

One month may not sound like a very long time. But when I think of everything that has found its way into these days, it feels like there is an entire little world between that first moment and where we are now.

A world made of laughter, late conversations, strange little jokes, quiet moments, and memories I already know I will keep close to my heart.

And perhaps that is my favourite thing about us. We did not know we were beginning something special. We were simply living through little moments, until one day we looked back and realised they had quietly become our story.

And this is only the first month of it.

Do you remember how it all started? 👀

I'm so thankful to you for everything, my cute little 'acting' femboy 🤭. And I fell in love shortly after :)

For Maryam

If I could gather every little moment that made me smile because of you, I think I would need a sky much larger than this one.

One moon hath wax'd since thou didst come,
And made my wandering heart thy home;
Yet in so brief a span of days,
Thou hast set all my nights ablaze.

Before thy light did cross my way,
The hours were but the common day;
But now each dawn doth sweeter rise,
As though thy name were in the skies.

Thy smile doth hold a gentler art
Than all the treasures of the heart;
And when thy laughter fills the air,
The world seems softer for it there.

If time would pause upon thy face,
I'd ask the stars to leave their place;
Let every wandering planet stay,
And steal no moment from that day.

What need have I of golden spheres,
Of crowns, of fame, of passing years?
Give me but thee, and I shall find
A richer kingdom in my mind.

Though but one month hath been our own,
My heart hath found what it had known
Not in the stars, nor written fate,
But in the joy of finding thee.

Yet if the heavens grant me grace,
And time should lead us place to place,
Through changing seasons, near or far,
I'd know thy light among the stars.

And should the years their chapters turn,
And countless other pages burn,
Let this first month forever prove
The tender beginning of our love.

For if I had but one request
Of fickle Fate, who knows the rest,
I'd ask, when all our days are through,
That I might still be drawn to you.

So let this little month remain,
A golden verse within time's reign;
A quiet proof, beneath the blue,
That once my heart found its way to you.

Uhibbuki Hubban Shadiidan, Maryam
